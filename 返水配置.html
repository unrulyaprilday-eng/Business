已开启

×

奖励所需打码量 = 返水奖励金额 × 打码倍数。可填 0 或正数，最多 1 位小数；0 表示无额外打码要求。

采用与月签到一致的时间设置方式，按运营地区对应的站点时区解释，不按当前电脑时区换算。每天此时结束上一返水周期，开始累计新周期有效打码量。

Q: How can I get the reward?

A: Become a VIP and meet the required effective betting amount to claim the reward after the next daily reset, using the configured reset time in the operating region's local time.

Q: How is the reward calculated?

A: Different VIP levels and effective betting amounts receive different reward rates. See the rebate rate details for the applicable rules.
